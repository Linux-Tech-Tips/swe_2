## Ubiquitous Language Glossary

Lecturer: Person who "owns" one or more modules, has access to all timetabling information of those modules.
Student: Person who participates in one or more modules, possesses own timetable.
TA: Special student, one or more of each must be present at each tutorial.
Module: Specific instance of a module, has a lecturer, students, and schedule.
Room: Location in which classes take place, have certain timetable, capacity, and equipment.
Buildings: Group of people in charge of rooms and their allocation.
Tutorial: Class for a module in addtion to lectures, there are typically one or more per week such that each student can attend at least one of them.


Nearly all words are in common across the different bounded contexts, only schedule differs in meaning:

Lecturers/TAs/Students: Schedule means their individual weekly timetable, when they are busy/free.
Room: Schedule means the weekly timetable of the room, when it is busy/free.
Module: Schedule is the collection of all schedules of all participants in the module (studetnts, lecturer, TAs)
Tutorial: Schedule of when each tutorial session is.
