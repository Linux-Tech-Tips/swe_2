\section{Project Description}

\subsection{Summary of Application Idea}
[Project Name] is an application designed to use mathematical graph theory to efficiently find the optimal timeslot(s) for tutorials in educational institutions. 
Its goal is to automate the complex yet tedious task of finding a suitable time to allocate a tutorial. The minimal viable product would have:

\begin{itemize}
\item Students and lecturers sign into the platform via their university email and fill out a weekly schedule indicating their availability.
\item Lecturers enter a list of student IDs from their module, with which the software would display one or more times that everyone in the module is available.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Intended Users and Solution Framework}
The intended users for [Project Name] are lecturers, students and other relevant staff in educational institutions. This project solves the prevalent and difficult 
problem of timetable conflicts for tutorials, especially for modules shared amongst multiple courses.
