\section{Project Description}

\textbf{Summary of Application Idea}\par
FACTS is an application designed to use mathematical graph theory to efficiently find the optimal timeslot(s) for tutorials in educational institutions. 
Its goal is to automate the complex yet tedious task of finding a suitable time to allocate a tutorial.\\

\textbf{Intended Users and Solution Framework}\par
The intended users for FACTS are lecturers, students and other relevant staff in educational institutions. This project solves the prevalent and difficult 
problem of timetable conflicts for tutorials, especially for modules shared amongst multiple courses.
\\The intended solution framework forsees the following features for a minimum viable product:

\begin{itemize}
\item Students and lecturers sign into the platform via their university email and fill out a weekly schedule indicating their availability.
\item Lecturers enter a list of student IDs from their module, with which the software would display one or more times that everyone in the module is available.
\end{itemize}
