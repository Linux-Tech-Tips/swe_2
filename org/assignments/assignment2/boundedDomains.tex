\section{Bounded Contexts and Domains}

The overarching domain is scheduling. For this application, the domain can be split into the following subdomains: \\
\textbf{Tutorial scheduling  (core domain)}: concerns itself with indivdual schedules, schedules of modules, the students taking modules, and the ability to access room information.\\
\textbf{Buildings (supporting subdomain)}: concerns itself with the capacity, availability, and equipment. \\

From this domain division flows a division into bounded contexts:\\

\textbf{Modules}: care about which students and lecturers particpate in them. \\
\textbf{Lecturers}: care about their own schedules, communicate with Modules to access information about the students taking those modules. They also communicate with Buildings to book suitable rooms. \\
\textbf{Students}: have their indivdual schedules, and communicate with Lecturers to access module schedule information. \\
\textbf{Buildings}: has information on the schedules/equipment/capacity of rooms, and communicates with Lecturers to use rooms efficiently.
